## Notes

- **Spaceship prices in USD.** The domain was $9.08, charged as €7.80
  at 1 USD = 0.858577 EUR on 2026-09-09. Every euro figure in this
  ledger for that vendor is therefore a snapshot, and a renewal at the
  same dollar price will land on a different euro amount.
- The first year took €0.67 off a €8.47 subtotal, so the renewal starts
  from the higher figure before any rate movement.
- **Auto-renew appears to be off** — the receipt recommends turning it
  on, which implies it is not. Worth confirming, since a lapsed
  registration loses the name.
- `paneloom.app` was available at €7.11 and deliberately **not** bought:
  a second TLD only earns its keep defensively, and nobody is competing
  for this name.
