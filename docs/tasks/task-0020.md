# Adapt the app shell to phones

On desktop and tablet widths (768px and up) the app is framed by a shell
taken from `docs/brand/landing-concept.png`: a white top bar with the mark,
the wordmark and a "Your data stays in this browser" badge, over a left
sidebar listing all nine sections with a line icon each
(`AppShell.tsx`, `TopBar.tsx`, `SideNav.tsx` in
`web/src/components/conditions/`). Below the stylesheet's
`max-width: 767px` — `MOBILE_QUERY` in `web/src/hooks/useIsMobile.ts` —
the shell deliberately renders the old wrapping top nav, `NavBar.tsx`,
unchanged. This task is the phone half of that design.

## Open question

How nine sections fit a phone: the sidebar as a drawer behind a menu
button, or a bottom tab bar (nine is too many for one; likely four or
five plus a "More"), and where the privacy badge goes when there is no
top-bar room for it.

## What must survive

- **Hide on scroll.** `useHideOnScroll.ts` slides the nav away reading
  down and back reading up; whatever replaces the top nav keeps that, or
  a bottom bar gets its own equivalent.
- **The results-table header reveal** from task-0015. `NavBar` publishes
  its height as `--mc-nav-h`, and `--mc-nav-offset` (zero while hidden)
  is what `TableScroller`'s pulled-open date header and its "Dates" chip
  park beneath. A drawer or bottom bar must still publish a top offset
  those overlays read, or they end up behind it.
- **Nav state.** The blocked state while diagnostic reports have errors
  (`isNavItemBlocked`), the route-derived active section
  (`isNavItemActive` — Panel Detail under Monitoring Panels, a report
  under Diagnostic Reports), keyboard access and `aria-current`.
- **No remount at the breakpoint.** `AppShell` keeps every slot in
  place whichever shell shows, so rotating a phone past 767px does not
  reset the page beneath; a phone shell must keep that property.
