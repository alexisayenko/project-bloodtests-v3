ranges carry `appliesTo: {sex}` and `ageLow`/`ageHigh`, but nothing
selects among multiple printed ranges yet (Ygia FSH/LH/E2 print five
female bands plus a male band per test). Pick the applicable range at
display time: sex from envelope meta, age = birthYear vs the report's
collectedAt. Fall back to showing the printed range text when neither
is set, per the interchange spec.
