# Reorganize the Reference Book into visual subsections

## Current structure

Everything lives in one file, `web/src/components/conditions/ReferenceBookPage.tsx`,
routed through a single component that switches on an optional `indexKey` param
(`#reference` for the landing page, `#reference/<key>` for every sub-page — one flat
namespace, no nesting in the URL itself).

Enumerated from the code:

- **Landing page** (`#reference`, `indexKey` unset) — the only place any grouping
  exists at all. It renders four `<h2>` section headers in a fixed order, each
  followed by a plain vertical list of clickable rows:
  - **Organism-wide aspects** — HP Axis (`#reference/hp-axis`), Testosterone
    (`#reference/testosterone`)
  - **Formulas and math** — Mass ↔ molar conversion (`#reference/molar-masses`),
    Units and how they are read (`#reference/units`)
  - **Analytes** — LOINC database (`#reference/loinc-database`), FSH
    (`#reference/fsh`)
  - **Indices and derived measurements** — 24 computed-index pages
    (`#reference/<index-key>`, e.g. `#reference/homair`, `#reference/cft`),
    sub-grouped under a *second*, unlabeled tier of `<h2>`-weight headers by each
    index's first monitoring panel (`d.panels[0]`): Cardiovascular Risk, Insulin
    Resistance, Hypogonadism, Adrenal, Hypothyroidism, Fatty Liver, Anemia,
    Pancreatic Function — 8 sub-groups, computed at render time from `INDEX_DEFS`,
    not hand-listed.
- **30 leaf pages** total: 6 one-off components (`HpAxisPage`, `TestosteronePage`,
  `MolarMassesPage`, `UnitsPage`, `LoincDatabasePage`, `FshPage`) plus 24
  `IndexDetail` renders, one per `INDEX_DEFS` entry.

Navigation is a flat list-and-detail pattern, not a sidebar and not tabs. Once on
a sub-page there is **no in-page way back** — no breadcrumb, no "Back to Reference
Book" link, no chevron (checked: none of these exist in the file). The only ways
back are the browser's Back button or re-clicking "Reference Book" in the app
shell's nav, which reloads the landing page from scratch and loses no state (there
is none to lose), but also gives no sense of *where in the book* you were. Every
sub-page is a bare `<h1>` in a `maxWidth` column — consistent with how Panel Detail
and report detail's sub-pages are styled, per CLAUDE.md, but that consistency is
exactly why the Reference Book itself doesn't stand out as a book with sections.

The CLAUDE.md prose already names four categories ("Indices and derived
measurements", "Organism-wide aspects", "Formulas and math", "Analytes") — but
that's a description of the file's section order, not a designed information
architecture. It matches the landing page's four `<h2>`s 1:1 because the prose was
written by reading the code, not the other way around.

## The problem

Structurally it's already grouped — the landing page is not a bare unordered list
of 30 links, credit where due. The real gaps:

1. **The grouping is decorative, not navigational.** The four `<h2>`s exist only
   on the landing page. They carry no identity of their own — no icon, no route,
   no color, nothing that shows up anywhere else in the app (unlike, say, panel
   tints in `panelMeta.ts`). A category is a heading, not a place.
2. **No persistent sense of location.** Once you're two clicks in — landing →
   "Indices and derived measurements" → "HOMA-IR" — nothing on screen says you're
   in the Reference Book at all, let alone in its Indices section. No breadcrumb,
   no sidebar highlighting the active category, no back link.
3. **The Indices group is already straining under its own weight.** 24 pages
   sub-grouped into 8 mini-sections *inside* one `<h2>` block, with no visual
   separation from the three other top-level categories above it other than
   whitespace. It reads as the dominant section by sheer length, not by design.
4. **It doesn't scale.** task-0034 adds two more axis pages (Thyroid/HPT,
   Adrenal/HPA) to "Organism-wide aspects" — fine, that's 2 more rows. But the
   pattern of "keep appending `<h2>` blocks and rows to one growing component" is
   already how we got a 1500-line file with a 24-entry section wedged in the
   middle. Nothing here breaks at 6 categories or 40 leaf pages, but nothing makes
   that scale *pleasant* either — it's linear list growth dressed as sections.

So: not "flat and unusable" — genuinely "flat where it should be structural."

## A concrete proposal

**Promote the four categories from headings to real sections**, each getting:

- A stable slug (`organism-wide`, `formulas`, `analytes`, `indices`) usable in the
  URL scheme (see Open questions).
- A one-line description (most already have one informally — e.g. the Indices
  section's intro paragraph — just not per-category).
- An icon + tint, the same pattern `panelMeta.ts` already uses for Monitoring
  Panels cards, so a category is visually identifiable at a glance rather than by
  reading its `<h2>` text.

**Landing page becomes a grid of four category cards** (reusing `Card` /
`CardParts.tsx` primitives already in the codebase — `CardHeader`, `IconBadge`,
`Overline` — rather than inventing new visual language), each card showing: icon,
category name, one-line description, and a count ("24 indices", "2 pages", "6
analytes"). This mirrors the Monitoring Panels grid's own card pattern, which
keeps the Reference Book visually consistent with the rest of the app instead of
introducing a third layout idiom.

**Each category gets its own landing sub-page** (`#reference/indices`,
`#reference/organism-wide`, etc.) rather than staying inline on the root page —
this is the part that actually solves the scaling problem. The Indices
category page keeps its existing panel-grouped sub-lists (Cardiovascular Risk,
Insulin Resistance, …) — that grouping is already good, it just needs a page of
its own instead of competing for space with three unrelated categories above it.
The other three categories are short enough (2–6 items each) to stay flat lists
on their own landing page, no further sub-grouping needed yet.

**Every leaf page gets a breadcrumb**: "Reference Book › Indices and derived
measurements › HOMA-IR", each segment a real link — root, then category page,
then (for Indices) optionally the panel sub-group if that turns out to be worth
a stop. This is the fix for gap #2 and costs one small shared component
(`ReferenceBreadcrumb` or similar) used by all 30+ leaf pages instead of each
inventing its own `<h1>`.

**Pages that don't obviously fit a category:**
- **FSH** (`#reference/fsh`) sits under "Analytes" today alongside the LOINC
  database, but it isn't a database entry — it's a single-analyte deep-dive (real
  vs. mislabeled PubChem structure, glycosylation figure). If more single-analyte
  pages get added later (plausible — FSH set a precedent), "Analytes" may need to
  split into "LOINC database" (the one big table) and "Analyte deep-dives" (FSH
  and future siblings) as two categories rather than one.
- **Units and how they are read** (`#reference/units`) is filed under "Formulas
  and math" but is arguably closer to "Organism-wide aspects" in spirit — it's
  about how the *app* reads data, not physiology or arithmetic. Leaving it under
  Formulas is defensible (it does derive factors, fold scales, etc.) but it's the
  one placement in the current four that's a judgment call rather than obvious.

## Open questions

1. **Does this change the `#reference/<key>` URL scheme?** Proposal above adds a
   *new* tier (`#reference/<category-slug>` for category landing pages) but
   leaves every existing leaf URL (`#reference/homair`, `#reference/fsh`, …)
   untouched — no leaf page needs to move under its category in the URL
   (`#reference/indices/homair`) for this to work, since `ReferenceBookPage`
   already resolves a leaf by `indexKey` alone regardless of nesting. Recommend
   keeping leaf URLs flat and unnested — it's less churn and nothing about the
   proposal requires the nesting.
2. **Does the Indices category page need its own further nav (tabs, filter) once
   it holds significantly more than 24 entries**, or does the existing
   panel-grouped flat list keep scaling fine? Punt until task-0034's two new axis
   pages and any future index additions show whether 24 was already near a
   practical ceiling for one scrolling page.
3. **Card counts on the landing grid** — do they recompute automatically from
   `INDEX_DEFS`/hand-counted arrays (matching this repo's consistent "derive,
   don't hand-list" pattern), or is a fixed number acceptable given how rarely
   categories other than Indices grow? Recommend deriving all four to avoid a
   fifth place these numbers can drift.
4. Should the "Organism-wide aspects" category card preview task-0034's two
   upcoming axis pages before they exist (i.e. is this task sequenced before or
   after task-0034)? Recommend after, so the category page ships with its real
   contents instead of needing a second pass.
