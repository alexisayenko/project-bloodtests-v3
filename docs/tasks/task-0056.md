# Source the calculated free testosterone 65 / 100 pg/mL cut-offs, or mark them unsourced

`cft` (Vermeulen) and `cftlh` (Ly & Handelsman) in `INDEX_DEFS`
(`web/src/data/indexDefs.ts`) both band on `cut: [100, 65]` pg/mL —
347 / 225 pmol/L — read as ">100 good · 65–100 low-normal · <65 low". Neither
index's references cite where those two numbers come from: the citations cover
the equations, the Endocrine Society's method recommendation and the
equilibrium-dialysis comparison, not the thresholds. That breaks the rule that
every reference range carries a cited primary source.

## What to do

- Find the primary source of each limit and cite it, with a short verbatim
  quote and a `retrieved` date. The 225 pmol/L lower limit is likely the
  European Association of Urology's male hypogonadism guideline — unverified.
- If no source holds up for a limit, say so in the index's own text, or drop
  the band, rather than keep a confident status on an uncited number.
