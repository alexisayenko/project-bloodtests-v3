# Reconcile the Hormonal Pathways estradiol feedback arrow with the spec

task-0024's spec draws estradiol's negative feedback as two dashed arrows,
E2 → ↓Kp at the hypothalamus's Kp neurons (ER-α) and E2 → ↓LH at the pituitary
([task-0024 §4.0](task-0024.md#40-edges)), dashed like every feedback and
crosstalk arrow. The build (a10e6d0) draws one solid arrow from blood E2 into
the brain image's right side, with a bare "↓" at its head: the Brain zone is
one image, with no Kp or LH node for the arrows to land on.

## What has to be decided

- Either the build moves to the spec — dashed, two targets — which needs Kp
  and pituitary LH to exist as drawn targets in the Brain zone, or at least as
  anchor points on the image; or the spec records the single solid arrow as the
  decision, with the reason.
- Whichever wins, the other feedback arrows still to come ([task-0059](task-0059.md))
  follow the same style.
