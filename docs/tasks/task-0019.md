# Record laboratory prices for the tests each laboratory sells

Goal: the app knows what a test costs at a given laboratory.

The request, in the user's words:

> laboratories prices

That is the whole of the intent captured so far. Everything below "What
exists today" is an open question, not a design — nothing here has been
decided.

## What exists today

- **A laboratory registry, unused.** `web/public/data/laboratories.json`
  lists laboratories — currently one, Synevo Ukraine (`synevo-ua`), with
  its `name`, `url` and `lang` translations and a `tests` map keyed by
  LOINC, each entry carrying the lab's own product `code`, printed `name`
  and product page `url` (two tests so far: TSH `11580-8` and calcitonin
  `1992-7`). It carries no prices. Nothing in `web/src` or `web/test`
  reads it, and unlike `analyses.json` / `panels.json` /
  `monitoring-panels.json` no schema in `web/public/schema/` describes
  it. It is the natural anchor for prices, but whether it is the right
  home is itself open (see Storage below).
- **A lab name per report.** Every diagnostic report in the interchange
  envelope carries a required `lab` string, "the laboratory's name, as
  printed" (`bloodtests-3.schema.json`; `docs/tech/interchange-format.md`),
  held in memory as the session's `place` (`parseUpload.ts`). It is free
  text, not a reference into `laboratories.json`, and the schema notes the
  same lab appears spelled several ways across years. The data in use
  comes from several laboratories — Cypriot and RU/CIS, per
  [`docs/brand/brief.md`](../brand/brief.md) — so any price lookup keyed on
  a report's lab first has to resolve those spellings to one laboratory.
- **A schedule with a month.** Panel Detail's Results tables and All
  Observations carry a "Scheduled" column: a per-row checkbox plus a month
  pill, stored as `{loincs, indices, month?}` under
  `bloodtests_scheduled_v1` (`web/src/components/conditions/scheduled.ts`).
  It says which tests are planned for the next draw, and when — but not
  where, and not at what cost.
- **First recorded prices, in markdown.**
  [`docs/content/laboratory-prices.md`](../content/laboratory-prices.md)
  holds Esculab's prices for ten products in UAH as of 2026-09-10, entered
  by the user. The app does not read this file.

## Why it belongs here

The schedule answers "what do I order next". Prices are what turn that
into a decision: the obvious payoff is costing the scheduled draw at each
laboratory and comparing them. That payoff is **a likely follow-up, not
part of this task** — and it would sit under the "Scheduler" draft in
[`docs/concerns.md`](../concerns.md) ("produce the order for my next
blood test"), which is not yet a numbered concern, so this task sets no
`concern`. Set one once that draft becomes a `Cn`.

## Open questions — resolve before building

- **What is priced: the analyte, the laboratory's bundle, or both?**
  Laboratories sell panels and bundles that cost less than their parts.
  Is a price attached to a single test, to a product that may contain
  several, or to both — and if both, does costing a set of tests have to
  find the cheapest combination of products that covers it?
- **How does a laboratory's product map to LOINC codes?** One product can
  cover several codes (a lipid profile), and one code can appear in
  several products (cholesterol alone, and inside that profile). The
  current `tests` map is LOINC → one product, which cannot express either
  direction. What does the mapping look like instead? The first recorded
  prices already show a harder case: a label can name a test without
  naming its method or specimen. Esculab's "LDL-C" could be the calculated
  code or the directly measured one. Its glucose could be serum/plasma or
  whole blood. Neither label picks a code, alongside the "FBC" bundle.
- **What currency, and at what scope?** Is a price per laboratory, per
  country the laboratory operates in, or per branch — given chains price
  differently by city or country?
- **Are prices dated?** Prices change. Is each price stamped with the date
  it was observed, versioned, or simply overwritten — and is the history
  kept, for instance to cost a past draw at the prices of its day?
- **Where do prices come from?** Manual entry by the user, a curated file
  shipped with the app, or fetched from laboratory websites? If fetched:
  what do the laboratories' terms of use allow, and who keeps it current?
  Note that fetching would be a network call, and the app currently makes
  exactly one, the explicit-opt-in NLM lookup isolated in
  `data/loincNlm.ts`.
- **Is it public reference data or private user data?** A curated file
  would live in the repo — which is public — next to
  `laboratories.json`. A price the user negotiated, or a discount they
  receive, is private and belongs in localStorage like the rest of their
  data. Does the app need one of these, the other, or both layered?
- **Does it travel with the reports?** Is price data part of the
  interchange envelope, Export / Import JSON and share-link payloads, or
  kept entirely separate? If the envelope: that is a format change and
  needs its own decision under
  [adr-0006](../tech/decisions/adr-0006-envelope-schema-numbered-3.md) and
  [adr-0012](../tech/decisions/adr-0012-envelope-version-is-a-major-minor-string.md).
- **How does a report's printed `lab` meet a registry entry?** Prices are
  keyed to a laboratory; reports name one as free text in several
  spellings. Is resolving that part of this task, or a prerequisite with
  its own?
