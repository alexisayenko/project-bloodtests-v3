# Research companion observations (draw-together pairs)

Goal: a per-marker `companions` list (LOINC codes) in
`web/public/data/analyses.json`, surfaced as a soft "draw together"
hint in the Scheduled column of Panel Detail's Analysis tab — beside
a scheduled observation whose companions aren't scheduled. No other
UI. Shape and boundaries are fixed in
[ADR-0005](../tech/decisions/adr-0005-companion-observations-are-not-panels.md)
and the
[companion observation](../product/concepts/companion-observation.md)
concept: a companion is a property of the marker, not of a
condition, and it never auto-schedules anything.

Approach: research first, catalog second. Per marker family, with
sources and verbatim quotes:

- iron studies (Ferritin with Iron / Transferrin / TIBC)
- B-vitamins with Homocysteine (B12 / Folate / B6)
- calcium/bone: Ca, Mg, vitamin D, PTH, phosphate
- thyroid (TSH with FT4)
- glucose/insulin
- lipids
- sex hormones

Findings land in a review doc, `docs/product/companions-research.md`,
and only after review does the `companions` list go into the catalog.
