# Strengthen the sourcing of the receptor effects in the Hormonal Pathways Effects cards

The receptor Effects cards read `web/public/data/pathway-receptor-effects.json`,
where every effect cites at least one retrieved source with a short verbatim
quote. Several rest on a single source:

- **Androgen receptors**: red blood cell production, body and facial hair and
  sebum, prostate growth, and male-pattern scalp hair loss cite only
  Handelsman's Endotext chapter, "Androgen Physiology, Pharmacology, Use and
  Misuse" — a review, not a primary study.
- **Estrogen receptors**: each effect cites one source — bone (Rochira &
  Carani 2009), body fat and sexual function (Finkelstein 2013), LH feedback
  (Hayes 2000), gynecomastia (Swerdloff & Ng's Endotext chapter).

## What to do

- Add a second retrieved source, primary where one exists, to each
  single-sourced effect, with its own short verbatim quote.
- The Endocrine Society's 2018 hypogonadism guideline (Bhasin et al., JCEM)
  covers several of these effects, but its full text was not openly
  retrievable when the cards were built; cite it only from a retrieved copy.
- An effect no second source supports stays as it is and is noted here.
