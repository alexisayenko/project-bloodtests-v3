# Rework monitoring panels — industry standard sets, condition mapping, custom panels, and export

The current monitoring panels (`monitoring-panels.json`) were assembled ad-hoc.
This task replaces them with an evidence-backed clinical taxonomy derived from
leading clinical laboratory practice (LabCorp, Quest, Mayo Clinic Laboratories,
ARUP, NHS, and international specialty guidelines), maps panels systematically to
clinical conditions, enables users to create and manage their own custom panels,
and ensures custom panels round-trip through the archive import/export.

---

## 1. Survey Top 20–30 Panels in Clinical Practice

Research and catalog the most widely ordered standard panels in diagnostic
laboratory medicine, categorized by physiological system and target condition:

1. **Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP)** — hepatic/renal/electrolytes/glucose.
2. **Basic Metabolic Panel (BMP)** — renal/electrolytes/glucose.
3. **Complete Blood Count (CBC / FBC) with Differential** — hematology, infection, marrow.
4. **Lipid Panel (Standard & Advanced)** — ASCVD risk (TC, HDL-C, LDL-C, Triglycerides, ApoB, Lp(a)).
5. **Thyroid Panel (Comprehensive)** — primary & secondary hypo/hyperthyroidism (TSH, FT4, FT3, Anti-TPO, Anti-Tg).
6. **Male Hormone / Hypogonadism Panel** — endocrine axis (Total T, Free T, SHBG, LH, FSH, Estradiol, Prolactin).
7. **Female Hormone / Menopause / Fertility Panel** — FSH, LH, Estradiol, Progesterone, AMH, Prolactin.
8. **Insulin Resistance & Glycemic Control Panel** — metabolic syndrome & diabetes (Fasting Glucose, Fasting Insulin, HbA1c, C-peptide, HOMA-IR).
9. **Liver Function Panel (LFT / Hepatic Function)** — hepatocellular & cholestatic injury (ALT, AST, ALP, Total Bilirubin, GGT, Albumin, Total Protein).
10. **Renal Function Panel** — acute/chronic kidney disease (Creatinine, eGFR, BUN/Urea, Uric Acid, Sodium, Potassium, Chloride, Bicarbonate, Albumin/Creatinine ratio).
11. **Iron Status & Anemia Panel** — iron deficiency vs. chronic disease (Serum Iron, Ferritin, TIBC, UIBC, Transferrin, % Transferrin Saturation).
12. **Cardiovascular & Inflammatory Risk Panel** — high-sensitivity CRP, Homocysteine, Lp(a), ApoB/A1.
13. **Bone & Mineral Metabolism Panel** — osteoporosis, hyperparathyroidism (Calcium, Ionized Calcium, Phosphorus, 25-OH Vitamin D, PTH, ALP, CTX, P1NP).
14. **Coagulation Panel** — bleeding/thrombosis (PT/INR, aPTT, Fibrinogen, D-Dimer).
15. **Adrenal / Cortisol Axis Panel** — HPA axis disorders (Cortisol AM/PM, ACTH, DHEA-S).
16. **Nutritional / Micronutrient Panel** — Vitamin B12, Folate, Vitamin D, Ferritin, Zinc, Magnesium.
17. **Pancreatic Function Panel** — pancreatitis & malabsorption (Amylase, Lipase, Elastase-1).
18. **Autoimmune / Rheumatology Screen** — ANA, RF, Anti-CCP, ESR, CRP.

## 2. Curate 10–15 Pre-Defined Standard Panels with Citations

Select 10–15 core condition-oriented panels built into the app by default.
Each pre-defined panel must document:
- **Condition association**: Clear link to the target physiological condition.
- **Clinical source citations**: Established guidelines (e.g. Endocrine Society, ADA Standards of Care, AHA/ACC Cholesterol Guidelines, KDIGO CKD Guidelines, AASLD Liver Guidelines, ATA Thyroid Guidelines).
- **Core vs. Extended marker tiers**:
  - **Core markers (5–7)**: Primary diagnostic markers displayed on summary cards.
  - **Extended markers**: Secondary differential/monitoring markers accessible in detail view.
  - **Derived clinical indices**: Applicable mathematical calculations (HOMA-IR, FIB-4, eGFR CKD-EPI, Atherogenic Index of Plasma, etc.).

---

## 3. User-Defined / Custom Panels

Allow users to tailor monitoring to their personal health protocol:
- **Create new panel**: User specifies panel name, target condition, accent color, icon, and optional description.
- **Marker picker**: Search and select any observation from the analyte catalog (by LOINC, friendly name, or short name) or computed index.
- **Edit & Reorder**: Add/remove markers, reorder cards, and adjust core vs. extended visibility.
- **Delete / Reset**: Remove custom panels or restore standard factory defaults.

---

## 4. Import / Export & Storage

- Custom panels are stored locally in the browser (`localStorage` / IndexedDB).
- **Archive bundle integration**:
  - Custom panel definitions included in the zip export (`custom-panels.json` or manifest entry in `web/src/data/backupArchive.ts`).
  - Strict round-trip validation upon import in `web/src/data/backupRestore.ts`.
  - Export → Import → Export byte-identical settlement.
