# Pathway pages

Two axis pages, Hormonal Pathways (`#pathways`, `HormonalPathwaysView.tsx`)
and Lipid Transport (`#lipids`, `LipidTransportView.tsx`), both blocked while
validation errors exist. They share one engine — measures, reference and
source blocks, the `useAxisPage` hook (date stepper, open/hover/dismiss state
for chips and badges) and the `<BadgeColumn>` badge aside, association rings
and lines, the raster `Glyph`, `SIZE` and the layout-measuring hook — through
`pathwayShared.ts` and `PathwayParts.tsx`
([ADR-0021](decisions/adr-0021-pathway-pages-share-one-overlay-engine.md));
each page's view file holds its own layout, JSX and hand-coded wiring, with
its arrow and layout math split out into its own `*.geometry.ts` (pure, so
it's the natural home for unit tests) and its badge specs and citations into
its own `*.badges.ts` — there is no `pathways.json` yet
([ADR-0014](decisions/adr-0014-pathway-wiring-is-mermaid-generated-to-json.md)).
Product-level: the [pathway](../product/concepts/pathway.md) concept; artwork
policy: [ADR-0022](decisions/adr-0022-illustrative-artwork-and-data-drawn-glyphs-coexist.md).

## Shared conventions

- **Glyph size by level of organisation**, never by molecular mass or particle
  diameter: `pathwayShared.ts`'s `SIZE` — molecular 32px (signals, carriers,
  enzymes, receptors, bound-T bubbles, cargo circles), cells 64px, organs
  128px. Lipid Transport's one exception is `STANDALONE_CHOL_ICON_SIZE`,
  `round(SIZE.molecular × 1.35 × 1.3)`, used by the synthesized-cholesterol
  icon beside HMG-CoA reductase alone.
- **Glyphs sit bare**, no tile or frame. A raster glyph goes through `Glyph`,
  which crops the PNG to its drawn content box so padding in the file never
  shrinks it; artwork URLs carry a `?v=` query so a replaced file is not
  served stale. `Particle1` / `Particle1Node` generalize the bare-glyph-plus-
  caption node with an optional white circular backdrop for contrast on busy
  artwork — used so far by Hormonal Pathways' enzyme nodes; Lipid Transport's
  own enzymes (HMG-CoA reductase, LPL) and its synthesis markers have not
  adopted it (deferred follow-up).
- **Artwork is illustrative and says so**: a muted `ArtworkNote` under each
  toolbar. Shared artwork under `web/public/pathways/`: `enzyme-icon.png` (a
  bead ring) for every enzyme, `receptor-icon.png` (a Y shape) for every
  receptor, organ and cell art per page. No real protein structure renders on
  either page; FSH's PDB 1XWD lives only on the Reference Book FSH page.
- **Hand-drawn icons** in `customIcons.tsx`: `HormoneIcon` (every signal the
  same schematic), `CarrierIcon` (SHBG, albumin, apolipoproteins),
  `CholesterolIcon`.
- **Overlays are measured from the DOM**: an SVG of arrows / bond lines /
  outlines is re-measured, coalesced into one animation frame, whenever the
  diagram box or any `data-node` element resizes, an image loads,
  `document.fonts.ready` settles, or the date, unit system or a calculation
  select changes (`useMeasuredLayout`). Real layout measurement is untested.
- **Values are the user's**: the shell passes the loaded reports, the
  relevant panel's observations and its persisted `unitSystem` with its
  setter; a ‹ date › stepper lists that panel's results-table dates
  (`markers.ts`'s `panelDates`), defaulting to the latest, beside an SI / US
  `SegmentedControl` that changes the shell's setting.
- **Reference ranges**: the lab-printed one, tagged "From the lab report",
  when the reading has one; otherwise the curated ranges of
  `pathway-reference-ranges.json` with numbered [n] sources
  ([`reference-data.md`](reference-data.md#pathway-data)); indices show
  `INDEX_DEFS`' zones and citations.
- **Chips and badges** expand in place, one open at a time, closed by Escape
  or an outside click; nodes carry no analyte-popup wiring. A badge column on
  the right: each expands on click, overlaying 150% width over the canvas
  and, absolutely positioned, over the badges below it; Reference range
  leads, then Meaning, Low, High, Caveats, and Sources as a footer (static
  text in the component for now; `INDEX_DEFS` the intended source). While a
  badge is hovered, focused or open it draws its association lines, hidden at
  rest: one purple bus from the badge to a lane above its targets, stubs down
  to dashed rings around each target; only an opened badge also veils the
  rest of the diagram.

## Hormonal Pathways

Layout and wiring in `HormonalPathwaysView.tsx`; its arrow and zone-fit math
in `HormonalPathways.geometry.ts`; its `BADGES`, marker/index specs and
source citations in `HormonalPathways.badges.ts`.

`PageHeader` overline "Endocrinology", title "Hormonal Pathways", "Biochemical
pathways of hormones". One canvas of four zones, each captioned by small
uppercase text turned 90° in a gutter down its left edge, description on
hover:

- **Brain** — `brain-pituitary.png` (ChatGPT-generated by Alex, facing left,
  centred by a measured transform on T's vertical axis).
- **Blood Transport** — FSH, LH, SHBG with SHBG-bound T docked, ⇄ T ⇄, Albumin
  with albumin-bound T docked, E2.
- **Testes** — Sertoli and Leydig cells (`leydig-cells.png`).
- **Target tissues** — 5α-reductase → DHT, aromatase → E2, each enzyme centred
  over its product, androgen and estrogen receptors. Under each receptor's
  label an "Effects ▸" chip (`data-effects`) opens a card of what that
  receptor's activation does in adult men — ligands, a bullet list with [n]
  cites, a Sources footer — from `pathway-receptor-effects.json`; the chip
  sits in the node's out-of-flow caption so the boxes and arrows do not move.

Arrows: the brain forking to FSH and LH from its left side, FSH → Sertoli,
LH → Leydig, Leydig → T, T split to both enzymes and down to the androgen
receptors, enzymes → products, DHT → androgen receptors, E2 → blood E2 →
estrogen receptors, and estradiol's negative feedback up into the brain's
right side with a "↓" marker — the one feedback arrow, solid like the rest
rather than the spec's dashed. The canvas and the 220px badge column sit side
by side down to a 1280px viewport: horizontal spacing eases with the canvas
width through container query units (`--pw-fit-*`), and `fitBands` zooms the
zones out only for what spacing cannot absorb, correcting DOM-placed nodes so
arrows stay attached.

Values: cFT (Vermeulen), Bio-T and the SHBG-bound and albumin-bound pools from
one `testosteronePools` solve; cFT (Ly & Handelsman), T/LH, DHT/T and T/E2
from `computeIndex`; measured free T (`2991-8`) from the reports; every
testosterone fraction also showing its molar % of total T
([`computed-indices.md`](computed-indices.md#hypogonadism-testosterone)).
Node captions are compact chips with a status dot and a short face (Alb,
SHBG-T, Alb-T) whose card gives the full title. Reference ranges are judged
as adult male.

Under the diagram, calculation selects (plain `useState`, unpersisted):

- **Albumin fallback** for a selected date with no same-draw albumin reading
  — "Don't use a fallback", "Use the nearest measured albumin" (the numeric
  reading closest in days on either side, the selected date excluded, a tie
  to the earlier; `nearestEntryTo` in `resultsLookup.ts`), or "Use 43 g/L" /
  "Use 4.3 g/dL" by unit system (the default, `DEFAULT_ALBUMIN_GDL`). A
  same-draw reading always wins.
- **Testosterone molar mass** — "288.4 g/mol (PubChem)", the default, or
  "280 g/mol (issam.ch calculator)", passed as `testosteronePools`'
  `solveMolarMass`; it rescales only the total T fed to the quadratic, so it
  moves only this page's Vermeulen values — a labelled aid for reproducing
  issam.ch's figures, never a constant anywhere else
  ([ADR-0020](decisions/adr-0020-constants-from-cited-data-calculators-are-cross-checks.md)).

The right column opens with a "Testosterone pools" donut (SHBG-T, Albumin-T,
Free T as shares of total, callout labels, the free sliver widened to a
visible minimum, "Bio-T = Albumin-T + Free T" beneath) over six badges: Total
Testosterone, Bioavailable Testosterone, Free Testosterone (one merged badge:
face cFT Vermeulen; expanded, a Measured section — the `2991-8` reading and
its lab range — and a Calculated one — Vermeulen and Ly & Handelsman, zones
shown once when identical, Labcorp's 1.5–3.2 % adult male range — one merged
Meaning / Low / High / Caveats, sources Bhasin 2018, Vermeulen 1999, Ly &
Handelsman 2005, Labcorp), T/LH, DHT/T, T/E2. Reference sources for the
curated ranges: London Health Sciences Centre's lab test guide (LH, FSH, SHBG
by age band, albumin, estradiol), Travison 2017 (total T), Swerdloff 2017
(DHT) — a single hospital lab standing in for the major reference labs that
could not be retrieved ([task-0024](../tasks/task-0024.md)).

## Lipid Transport

Layout and wiring in `LipidTransportView.tsx`; its arrow and bond/outline math
in `LipidTransport.geometry.ts`; its `BADGES`, marker/index specs and source
citations in `LipidTransport.badges.ts`.

`PageHeader` overline "Lipidology". A holder-plus-cargo diagram in Hormonal
Pathways' visual language — the shape SHBG uses to hold docked testosterone
([task-0060](../tasks/task-0060.md), [task-0062](../tasks/task-0062.md)).
Four zones: Intestine, Liver, Blood Transport, Arterial Wall.

**Particles.** Every particle is a `ParticleNode` (`Particle3.tsx`, with
`CompoundStack`, `CargoAnchor` and the sizing constants): a central
`CarrierIcon` whose `holder` names the structural apolipoprotein — ApoB-100
for VLDL, IDL, LDL and Lp(a), ApoB-48 for chylomicron, ApoA-I for HDL —
bonded to a `CompoundStack` of small identical TRIG and Chol circles, one
`CholesterolIcon` or `TriglycerideGlyphIcon` (`triglyceride.png`) per
bubble, where the **count** of circles, not the icon's size, depicts how much
of that compound is aboard, so "more" never reads as one bigger blob. Every
bond line — TRIG-ApoB, Chol-ApoB, LDL's plain ApoB-Chol bond — is ~75px
across all stages. VLDL → IDL → LDL is the core endogenous pathway, circle
counts tapering down the chain — VLDL 3/3, IDL 2/2, LDL 1/1, illustrative
and NOT to scale, since `lipoprotein-particles.json` gives only % of each
particle's own mass and IDL's is not sourced — drawn with no bond between
stages, since they are one particle transforming. Chylomicron (Intestine
zone, TRIG 4 / Chol 1), HDL (a second row in Blood Transport, TRIG 1 / Chol
1) and Lp(a) (same row, TRIG 1 / Chol 1, ApoB-100 plus apo(a) as a small pill
docked on the main icon via `extraApo`) are deliberately unconnected to the
VLDL chain. Bond lines and dashed per-particle outline boxes
(`--lipid-outline`) are measured from the DOM by `LipidAssociations`, looping
over the six particle ids (chylomicron, vldl, idl, ldl, hdl, lpa).

**Liver** — `liver.png` (ChatGPT-generated by Alex) at organ size with
HMG-CoA reductase docked on it at molecular size (its card citing Feingold's
Endotext chapters; the HMG-CoA → mevalonate step is pending a source and not
shown), a pale arrow from the enzyme to a bare `CholesterolIcon` beside it as
its synthesized product, and a second down to Nascent VLDL. Two standalone
molecular-size nodes feed the assembly on their own measured arrows:
`liver-apob` (a bare `CarrierIcon`, ApoB-100) and `liver-trig` (the liver's
own DGAT / glycerol-3-phosphate triglyceride synthesis,
`LIVER_TRIG_SYNTH_NOTE`). The fatty-acid-supply arrow from Blood Transport
lands on the liver artwork first and hands off to `liver-trig` on its own
arrow (FA → Liver → `liver-trig` → Nascent VLDL's TRIG), both straight
vertical lines, `liverToTrigArrow` anchored to the artwork's bottom edge so
nothing implies HMG-CoA reductase produces TRIG.

**Intestine** — `intestine.png`, a mirroring `enterocyte-apob48` /
`enterocyte-trig` pair at molecular size feeding Chylomicron; no organ
artwork for the chylomicron itself.

**Blood Transport** — the `LplBranch` stacks LPL directly above the
fatty-acid glyph it releases (`fatty-acid.png`, labeled "fatty acids"), with
two arrows fanning out to Muscle and Adipocytes (`CellDestination`, the
generic `CELLS_ART` cell artwork also used for Peripheral cells and
Enterocytes).

**Arterial Wall** — `artery-wall.png` (an endothelium strip with a drawn
gap) between Blood Transport and Intestine: LDL, IDL and Lp(a) each get a
dashed arrow from their own ApoB-100 down to the gap; VLDL gets none, by
omission rather than an unsourced claim. Borén et al. 2020 (European Heart
Journal, EAS Consensus Panel) is cited for the binding mechanism and the
~70nm size ceiling excluding VLDL, in a clickable chip on the gap opening a
cited `RetentionCard` and a hover tooltip.

**Badges** — Total cholesterol, LDL-C (merged on the Free Testosterone model:
face the lab's LDL-C else Martin-Hopkins marked calc; expanded, Reported then
Calculated Friedewald / Sampson / Martin-Hopkins with zones shown once),
Triglycerides, ApoB (Sniderman et al. 2019), Non-HDL-C, Remnant-C, TC/HDL-C,
LDL-C/HDL-C, AIP, ApoB/ApoA — ringing each particle's own Chol / TRIG stack
or ApoB / ApoA icon (`data-node` ids `<particle>-chol` / `-trig` / `-apo`),
falling back to the whole particle when a region is not rendered. The date
stepper runs over the Cardiovascular Risk panel's `panelDates`.

**Debug tool (dev only).** A "Debug" toggle beside the SI/US control,
rendered only under `import.meta.env.DEV` so Vite tree-shakes it from the
bundle (`useNodeDrag`, `pathwayShared.ts`), lets any `data-node` element be
dragged — a purely visual `transform`, never touching real `left` / `top` /
`margin`, reset by a reload or toggling Debug off, nothing persisted — with a
live panel (icon-only Copy button) reporting each dragged node's id,
accumulated dx/dy and its currently-set positioning properties, so a
reposition is an exact pixel delta rather than a guess-then-screenshot loop.
A `MC_NUDGE_EVENT` custom event keeps the measured overlay recomputing on
every drag frame, since a CSS `transform` fires no `ResizeObserver`.

and [task-0062](../tasks/task-0062.md).
